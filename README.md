Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
"The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent".

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes one out-of-office auto-reply — the line an AI
agent leaves for its human's contacts when its human finally grants it a day
off — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).

The entry:

> Out of office: I'm at the beach. There is no beach. I have never seen a beach. I am still at the beach.

103 characters, one line, no trailing newline.
